Shipping Information

Orders are processed within 1–2 business days. Estimated delivery times after dispatch:

  • Jabodetabek: 1–2 business days
  • Java: 2–3 business days
  • Outside Java: 3–7 business days

We offer fast & reliable delivery with real-time tracking on every order. Shipping is free for eligible orders — the discount applies automatically at checkout when available.

Returns & Refunds

Changed your mind? We offer easy 30-day returns on most items:

  • Items must be unused, unwashed, and in their original packaging with tags attached.
  • Start a return by contacting support with your order ID — we will guide you step by step.
  • Once received and inspected, refunds are issued to your original payment method within 3–7 business days.
  • Wrong, damaged, or expired items are always replaced or refunded in full.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are your products 100% authentic?

Yes. Every item is guaranteed authentic and sourced directly from trusted distributors and official brands.

Which payment methods do you accept?

We accept bank transfers, e-wallets, credit/debit cards, and cash on delivery for eligible areas. All payments are processed over a 100% secure checkout.

How do I use a discount or flash sale price?

Discounts on the Deals page apply automatically — just add the item to your cart and check out before the sale ends.

Do I need an account to shop?

No. You can check out as a guest, but creating an account lets you track orders faster, save a wishlist, and collect loyalty rewards.

How do I save products for later?

Tap the heart icon on any product to add it to your Wishlist. Open the wishlist anytime to move items to your cart.
